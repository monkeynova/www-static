SCRAP BEAST — Survive 100 Days
==============================

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Just double-click "index.html" — it opens in any web browser
(Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No install, no internet needed.
Click the game once so it can play sound, then press ENTER.

CONTROLS
--------
  WASD / Arrow keys ... move
  SPACE .............. slam attack
  SHIFT .............. EMP blast (needs a Generator Core)
  B .................. open the shop (forge upgrades)
  X .................. inside the shop, switch shops
                       (Black Market, and the Secret Vault on day 75)
  ENTER .............. advance dialogue / menus / restart

THE GAME
--------
You are a beast made of scrap metal, built in a lab to destroy the
world — but you'd rather defend it.

1. Escape the lab: knock scrap loose and slip out while the
   scientists aren't watching.
2. Survive 100 days in a dinosaur-age jungle. Fight off human
   armies, collect scrap, and forge upgrades (armor, speed,
   weapons, a generator core, tentacles, a rail cannon, mortars).
3. Build a pack, raze or shelter in towns and cities, fell trees
   for wood, and mine diamonds.
4. On day 75 a Secret Vault opens — buy the OMEGA CORE with
   diamonds. It's the ONLY thing that can hurt the final boss.
5. On day 100 the LAB-MECHA attacks. Destroy it to win.

Have fun!
